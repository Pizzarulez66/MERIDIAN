# Progression and endgame — Project MERIDIAN

## Campaign structure

Progression is milestone-based, not a strict linear machine checklist. Each chapter introduces a new industrial capability, teaches why it matters, and then asks the player to automate it before moving on.

### Chapter 0 — Survey and salvage

**Purpose:** turn the world into a resource map.

- Locate ore-bearing regions and establish the first worksite.
- Introduce IE's mineral survey/excavation as a strategic long-term resource source.
- Establish safe bulk storage and transport before machine sprawl begins.
- Early Mekanism devices are introduced as precision tools, not as a full bypass of IE's early metallurgy.

**Milestone:** a documented resource site and a stable source of basic power.

### Chapter 1 — The Foundry Charter

**Purpose:** establish the first shared material economy.

- IE Coke Oven, Blast Furnace, Alloy Kiln, and early component crafting establish coke, steel, and alloy supply.
- Mekanism Enrichment Chamber, Energized Smelter, Metallurgic Infuser, and basic cables establish the first powered machine line.
- Players route at least one IE-produced bulk material into a Mekanism machine and use a Mekanism-produced intermediate in an IE fabrication step (the recipes will be explicit, not merely suggested).

**Milestone:** a working two-mod steel/component line with stock buffers.

### Chapter 2 — Bulk to precision

**Purpose:** industrialize ore processing.

- IE Excavator/Crusher outputs become the feedstock for Mekanism ore-processing stages.
- Introduce Mekanism 3x ore processing as the first serious chemical/power investment.
- IE Metal Press and Assembler turn refined outputs into standardized plates, frames, and machine components.
- Teach throughput planning: input buffering, output overflow, and machine bottleneck diagnosis.

**Milestone:** an automated ore-to-component production line that uses both mods.

### Chapter 3 — The Chemical Works

**Purpose:** add process chemistry and specialized materials.

- Add Electrolytic Separator, Chemical Infuser, Pressurized Reaction Chamber, and the required gas/fluid handling.
- Introduce selected 4x processing routes as expensive specialist production, rather than applying maximum multiplication to every ore.
- Bring Immersive Petroleum online as a separate liquid-fuel industry.
- Use explicit recipes to turn advanced Mekanism chemical products into feedstock for IE's Arc Furnace/Assembler lines.

**Milestone:** a continuously supplied chemical plant and at least one advanced component line.

### Chapter 4 — The Power Spine

**Purpose:** design power as infrastructure rather than a single generator upgrade.

- IE wind/water and fuel generation provide distributed or backup power.
- Mekanism generators and an Induction Matrix provide scalable generation and storage.
- Introduce IE high-voltage distribution between plant districts and Mekanism local buses within each district.
- Add fission only after players have automated fuel, coolant, waste handling, monitoring, and a fail-safe power plan.

**Milestone:** a stable, redundant network that can black-start after a simulated shutdown.

### Chapter 5 — Regional industry

**Purpose:** move from a workshop to an industrial region.

- Establish separate Resource, Chemical, Power, and Assembly districts.
- Use physical transport and buffering to connect the sites; optional Create trains can extend this to distant deposits.
- Use Mekanism factories for repeatable processing, while retaining IE's multiblocks for high-value metallurgy and fabrication.
- Introduce optional AE2 only as a planning/storage layer after physical networks are functioning.

**Milestone:** every district delivers a measurable, sustained intermediate to the next one.

## The endgame — Project MERIDIAN: Commission the Industrial Grid

The final objective is to build a functioning regional industrial system with four districts and a central commissioning plant. It should feel like a megaproject that players designed and operated, not a shopping list.

### District A: Resource Basin

- Operate at least one IE Excavator on a chosen mineral vein.
- Move its output through an IE bulk-handling line into Mekanism processing.
- Buffer raw feed and processed products separately; demonstrate that the line does not back up under continuous operation.

### District B: Chemical Works

- Run a Mekanism chemical-processing line capable of producing the advanced materials selected for the final bill of materials.
- Include chemical input production, storage, and automated distribution.
- Send a real product stream to the IE foundry; do not substitute a manually carried one-off stack for the factory link.

### District C: Power Station and HV Spine

- Commission a Mekanism Induction Matrix and an advanced power source appropriate to the chosen world/server balance.
- Build an IE high-voltage trunk that physically links the power district to the other industrial districts.
- Include an IE fuel-based black-start generator and a safe restart procedure.
- Demonstrate power transfer in both mod directions during the test phase; use the measured configuration in the final quest guide.

### District D: Central Foundry

- Operate an IE Arc Furnace, Metal Press, and Assembler as a component-production chain.
- Feed them with Mekanism-refined materials and chemical products.
- Use Mekanism factories and transporters for subassembly and machine feed.
- Produce the final **Grid Synchronizer** in the IE Assembler from a bespoke pack recipe. Its exact item ingredients are deliberately pending registry validation and balance testing.

### Commissioning checklist

The final quest chapter will require:

1. The Grid Synchronizer item.
2. A Resource Basin task that detects the relevant IE/Mekanism products.
3. A Chemical Works task that detects the chosen Mekanism end-product.
4. A Power Spine task that checks crafted hardware and includes a player-certified build checklist.
5. A Central Foundry task that checks the final component outputs.
6. A final self-audit: the player confirms that all four districts are physically linked and that the production line runs without manual item hand-carrying.

The book will clearly label the build checklist as self-certified. Without a bespoke structure/power-network validator, FTB Quests cannot automatically verify wire routing, machine adjacency, or sustained throughput.

### Rewards and postgame

- Main reward: a unique cosmetic/commemorative MERIDIAN core or display item, plus a final chapter recording the commissioned system.
- Optional challenge: expand the Resource Basin to multiple veins and add rail freight.
- Optional challenge: build a second, isolated power island with independent black-start capability.
- Optional challenge: upgrade selected materials to 4x/5x processing where the performance and chemical overhead are justified.
- Optional challenge: design a waste-minimizing chemical plant and document its byproduct loops.

The main completion condition remains the regional grid. Optional challenges should extend the factory, not add arbitrary crafting chores.
